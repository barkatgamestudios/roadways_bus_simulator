Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Roadways Bus Simulator (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), a mobile game developed and provided by Barkat Game Studios (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider"). This policy explains how information may be handled when you download or use the Application.

What Information Does the Application Obtain and How Is It Used?

User-Provided Information

The Application does not require users to create an account or register to play. You are not required to provide personal information to access its basic gameplay features.

If you contact the Service Provider by email, the information you include in your message, such as your email address and message content, may be used to respond to your inquiry.

Automatically Collected Information

Depending on the services and software development kits integrated into the Application, certain technical or usage information may be collected automatically. This may include device information, operating system details, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, and application interaction data.

The information collected depends on the features and third-party services enabled in the version of the Application you use. Please review the relevant third-party privacy policies listed below for further details.

Does the Application Collect Precise Location Information?

The Application does not require precise real-time location information for its core bus simulation gameplay. It does not intentionally collect precise GPS location through its own gameplay features.

However, third-party services integrated into the Application may process certain information according to their own configurations and privacy policies.

Advertisements

The Application may display advertisements to support its development and maintenance. Advertising services, including Google AdMob where integrated, may process device information and advertising-related data as described in their privacy policies and according to the applicable consent and advertising settings.

Non-Personalized Advertisements

If non-personalized advertising is enabled, advertisements are not personalized based on a user's advertising profile. However, limited information may still be processed for purposes such as ad delivery, frequency capping, reporting, fraud prevention, and compliance with legal requirements.

The type of advertising shown may depend on the Application's AdMob configuration, applicable consent requirements, regional rules, and the user's settings.

We do not claim that all advertisements are non-personalized unless this is consistently enforced by the actual advertising configuration used by the Application.

Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services to provide advertising, platform functionality, analytics, diagnostics, or other technical features. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Examples of relevant service providers include:

Children's Privacy

The Application's intended audience and age rating are determined by its Google Play listing and the developer's distribution settings. Parents and guardians should review the applicable rating and help children use mobile applications appropriately.

Where the Application is directed to children or is used by children in circumstances covered by applicable privacy laws, the Service Provider will configure its practices and applicable third-party services in accordance with the relevant legal and platform requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a manner that requires action under applicable law, please contact us at contact.barkatgs@gmail.com.
